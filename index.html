Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

At DocStudio, we believe confidential document manipulation should never require handing your private files to third-party cloud servers. This Privacy Policy details our air-gapped, client-side cryptographic architecture and data practices.

1. The Zero-Retention Client-Side Architecture

Unlike conventional web document converters that upload your PDFs to remote cloud machines, DocStudio operates entirely inside your local browser memory using WebAssembly (WASM) and HTML5 Canvas APIs. Your documents, scanned images, contracts, and financial statements are NEVER transmitted to our backend servers or stored on remote disks.

2. Information We Process

  • Document Data: Read exclusively in client-side RAM (Blob/ArrayBuffer). Once your browser tab or session is closed, all working files are permanently discarded from memory.
  • Authentication & Profile Data: When you sign in with Google, we receive only your public Google identifier (Name, Email, Profile Avatar) via secure Google Identity Services to maintain your session and manage your subscription privileges.
  • Subscription & Billing Records: For UPI transactions, we record only the transaction timestamp, chosen plan duration (Daily ₹5, Monthly ₹100, Yearly ₹1,000), payment method, and optional user-submitted UTR reference to verify and grant your Pro status.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

DocStudio utilizes browser localStorage strictly for necessary functional operations, including your preferred theme (Dark/Light mode), anonymous daily operation counter, and your temporary download history list. No cross-site ad tracking cookies are deployed.

4. Contact for Privacy Inquiries

If you have any questions or data requests regarding our zero-retention privacy protocols, contact our Data Governance Officer at support@docstudio.com or apnacollegebihar@slc.

About DocStudio

Our Mission & Engineering Vision

DocStudio was created with a clear objective: to challenge the centralized cloud monopolies of the document management industry.

For over a decade, individuals and businesses were forced to upload confidential legal agreements, tax filings, and personal identification to opaque remote servers just to merge a PDF or convert a Word document.

By leveraging cutting-edge WebAssembly (WASM), compiled C++ PDF engines, and modern browser multithreading, DocStudio brings the entire processing suite directly into your local machine. No waiting in remote server queues. Zero latency. Absolute air-gapped security.

Core Engineering Principles:
• 100% In-Browser Privacy: Your sensitive files never touch external disks.
• Decentralized Speed: Instantaneous conversions powered by your device's CPU.
• Accessible Pricing: From ₹5 daily passes to ₹100 monthly pro, democratizing document productivity for everyone.
